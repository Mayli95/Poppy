# Copy — Poppy

Voice: declarative, short sentences, calls the product what it does before it calls it what it is. Never hedges ("might help," "gently"). Uses the customer's own words from research — kombucha described by intensity before flavor, delivery described in days and clock times, not vague promises.

## About page

**Headline:** "We used to press this at night and sell it until it ran out."

**Body:**
"Poppy started on a cargo bike at Torgallmenningen, pressing overnight in a rented kitchen in Møhlenpris and selling from a cooler until we ran out — which was most days by eleven. We're in Solheimsviken now, with our own fridges and a production board on the wall that's sorted into four columns: Wake Up, Refuel, Cool Down, Reset. Everything we make goes on that board before it goes anywhere else, including this site. We don't sort by juice or kombucha or smoothie because our customers never did either — nobody at the fridge says 'I'd like a fermented tea,' they say 'I need to not feel like this anymore.'

We press cold, which means no heat touches any of it — and it also means everything has a real, short life. Seven days from the press, stamped on the cap the day it happens. If a drink's been sitting around longer than that, we'd rather throw it out than sell it to you. That's not a marketing line, it's just the chemistry of not pasteurizing anything.

Flavors move with what's actually good: sea buckthorn shows up for about ten weeks in autumn, rhubarb takes over in June, black carrot turns up in the dead of January because that's when it's the only interesting thing left in a Bergen greenhouse. The core eight don't move. Everything else does."

**Trade line (footer, kept small on purpose):**
"A few Bergen cafés and two gyms take weekly crates from us. If that's you, get in touch."

## Delivery & Returns

**Headline:** "The part everyone gets wrong is the day, not the drink."

**Delivery:**
"Order by 14:00 and we pack it that evening. Bergen city, Åsane and Fyllingsdalen: it's at your door the next day. Rest of Vestland: give it two days. We don't send anything further north than that, and we're not going to pretend a box can sit in a sorting depot for three days and still be worth drinking — it can't, so we don't sell you the lie.

Everything ships cold, in an insulated box, on the days we're actually pressing — order Friday afternoon and your box leaves Monday, not Saturday, because nobody's in the kitchen on a Saturday to pack it right. The checkout will tell you your actual delivery day before you pay, not after.

Every bottle has its press date stamped on the cap. Seven days from that date, in your fridge, unopened. Once it's open, drink it that day — this isn't the kind of juice that waits around."

**Returns:**
"We can't take a bottle back once it's left our fridge — it's unpasteurized, it's been through a delivery box, and we have no way of knowing what happened to it between our door and yours. If something arrived broken, warm, or just wrong, tell us within 24 hours with a photo and we'll replace it or refund it, no argument and no form to fill in. If you ordered the wrong flavor and it's still sealed and cold, that's on us to sort out too. What we won't do is take back something you've opened, tasted, and decided you didn't like — that's not a returns policy, that's just us drinking your leftovers, and we press enough of our own."

## Microcopy — buying flow

- **Add to cart confirmation:** "In the box: [Product Name]. Still need six-pack pricing? You're one bottle away."
- **Cart, six-pack nudge (shown when cart has 3-5 single bottles of the same hero juice):** "You're two bottles off six-pack pricing on [Product Name] — same box, less per bottle."
- **Empty cart:** "Nothing in here yet. Pick a state — that's faster than picking a flavor."
- **Delivery postcode outside Vestland (error state):** "We don't send this far — it's a seven-day product and we're not going to pretend a courier can outrun that. Sorry. If you're ever in Bergen, come find the fridge."
- **Order placed after 14:00 cutoff (notice, not an error):** "You're in — this one leaves tomorrow evening instead of today, so it still arrives properly cold."
- **Order confirmation:** "Pressed this week, boxed cold, on its way. You'll know the exact day before it leaves, not after."
- **Out of stock on a seasonal item:** "[Product Name] sold through for this run. It'll be back — ask us when, we actually know."
